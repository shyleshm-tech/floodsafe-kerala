🧮 Road Safety Scoring Model

Overall Road Safety Score = Σ (AHP Weight × Normalized Parameter Score)

Higher score means higher route safety. Distance is normalized so that the shorter route receives the higher distance-safety score. For the other parameters, safer conditions receive higher scores.
